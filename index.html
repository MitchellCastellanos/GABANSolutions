Case study

RCR Barber Shop: one brand, one system

RCR needed a professional online presence that matched its premium identity — one that made it easy for clients to explore services, see hours, and book a visit.

We built a branded multi-page site with booking, a gallery and a review-driven structure. Behind it, an internal admin system tracks the product catalog, reads QR codes and barcodes, and logs daily transactions.

The result is one system that carries both sides of the business: the brand clients see, and the operations behind the counter.
