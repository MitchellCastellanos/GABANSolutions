Reptiles Concept

Custom e-commerce platform for a Quebec reptile store that used to sell exclusively through Facebook. Built to handle a mixed catalog of unique living animals alongside standard accessory products, with direct online checkout and real-time sync to the shop's in-store Clover terminal.

Client need

Reptiles Concept relied entirely on Facebook to sell — no website, no online payments, no customer history, and a real risk of selling the same animal twice with no centralized inventory. They needed one platform to unify their live-animal catalog, online sales, and in-person sales at their Clover terminal.

What we built

A bilingual (FR/EN) site with a live-animal catalog (genetics, lineage, real-time availability), an accessories store, direct-payment checkout with automatic GST/QST tax calculation, in-store pickup with order-status tracking, customer accounts with wishlist and order history, and a full admin panel — mini-CRM, unified financial ledger, email campaigns, analytics — synced in real time with the in-store Clover POS.

Client Project

Montreal Spider Co.

Bilingual e-commerce and operations platform for a Montreal-area breeder of captive-bred tarantulas, built to sell live, individually-priced specimens alongside care products.

Client need

Montreal Spider Co. needed a storefront that could sell live, individually-priced specimens alongside care products, coordinate deliveries within Montreal and pickup points, and manage consignment relationships with distributor partners — without losing track of which specimen was where.

What we built

A bilingual (EN/FR) storefront with a species catalog (care info, size, price, availability, ratings), filters by experience level, gender, temperament, type and price, individual product pages, a persistent cart, checkout with Montreal delivery zones and pickup points, estimated GST/QST, user accounts, care guides and FAQ. Behind it, an operations panel tracks each specimen's lifecycle, warehouse location, consignment and transit status, store audits, restock proposals, a settlement ledger with partners, growth and molt history, per-specimen pricing, printable labels and QR-code operations, plus a bilingual notification service with delivery-attempt logging.

Client Project

Garage Carlos A Inc.

Public, mobile-first website for a real auto repair shop in Montreal, with online booking and self-serve appointment confirmations — built on top of the GarageOS platform above.

Client need

Garage Carlos needed a professional website where customers could see its services and book an appointment themselves, in their own language — instead of tying up the phone lines all day and risking double-booked or overloaded time slots.

What we built

A multilingual (EN/FR/ES) public website — hero, services, shop info — with a booking page where customers pick an open slot and book without creating an account. After booking, they get a private manage link by email/SMS to confirm or cancel their own appointment, no login needed. A WhatsApp button gives a direct line for anything else. The booking calendar checks real-time availability so two customers can never grab the same slot, and spreads appointments evenly across the day instead of stacking them all on the shop's busiest hours.
