Why it's priced this way

A site can be owned or rented. SEO and ads can only be rented.

A website is a thing — you can pay for it once and keep it, or pay less up front and cover the ongoing hosting and edits monthly. SEO and ad management aren't things, they're work that has to keep happening. Selling them as a single payment would mean the work stops the day you pay — so we don't sell them that way.

  • No surprise invoices — every price on this page is the full price
  • Mix and match — start with a site, add SEO or ads later
  • Custom systems outside this catalog are quoted separately
